Hi, this is the personal website of Nicholas Rees.
I am a Masters student at the University of Bonn, studying pure mathematics. My focus is largely in number theory and algebraic geometry, and I have a particular interest in those problems with ideas/problems coming from more analytic (number theoretic) origins. In my masters thesis, I am currently looking at q-deformations of differential equations originating from conformal field theory through the study of Habiro cohomology. Some other interests are in the applications of algebraic geometry to the study of trace functions, and the Langlands program (broadly construed). In the past, I have been interested in higher-order Fourier analysis, and Kakeya / restriction type problems in harmonic analysis.

External to mathematics, I am also interested in formal proof assistants and programming languages, as well as topics in AI safety. Outside of academics, I enjoy rock climbing and hiking in nearby mountains.
